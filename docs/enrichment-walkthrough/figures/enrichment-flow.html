The enrichment flow, as one diagram

A high-level flow of the enrichment map for slides: six steps, one decision, two ways a record goes back to SAP, the three rules behind every step, and what runs after the API.

Saying it in 30 seconds

Every SAP record is first cleaned, then identified against the registries, and its department found. If anything is still unknown, we read the web, but the answer must come from the pages and pass a registry.

Every record is then verified and judged: it goes back to SAP either trusted, or flagged for a data steward. Nothing is invented along the way.
